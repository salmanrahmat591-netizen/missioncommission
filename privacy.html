Privacy

Mission Commission is a free practice-test website. It has no accounts and does not send your answers or scores to any server.

  • On your device: your test scores, an unfinished test, your unlocked courses and a random device code are saved in your browser's storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • WhatsApp requests: when you request access to a course, WhatsApp opens with a message to the site owner. Your WhatsApp number and that message are shared with the owner through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. They are only used to reply to your request.
  • Fonts: the page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.
