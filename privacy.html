Privacy

Mission Commission is a practice-test app. It does not have user accounts and it does not send your answers to a server.

What stays on your device

Your name (if you enter it at login), your test results, which courses you have unlocked and until when, and a random device code. These are saved in your browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What leaves your device

When you request a course, the app opens WhatsApp with a message that contains the course name and your device code. You decide whether to send it. The owner receives only what you send. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Access links

An access link from the owner contains a signed code for one course and one device. It unlocks that course for the period the owner chose.
